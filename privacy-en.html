Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

0. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller for your personal data is Sergio de la Peña, an individual rather than a company.

For anything to do with your personal data, including exercising the rights described below, the contact point is foodranker.app@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

When you use FoodRanker we collect:

  • Your Google account details (name, email address and profile photo) when you sign in.
  • Photos of dishes that you choose to upload.
  • Ratings, comments and the name and address of the venue you enter when posting a dish.
  • Your approximate or precise location, only if you grant permission and only while you are searching for nearby venues (see point 2).
  • A device identifier so we can send you notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging token).
  • Your device language, so we can write notifications in it.
  • Usage and diagnostic data: screens visited, basic events and crash reports.

2. Location

FoodRanker asks for location permission for one purpose only: to find the venues near you when you post a dish, so you do not have to type the address by hand.

  • The permission is optional. If you decline it you can still use the app and find the venue by typing its name.
  • Your location is used at the moment of the search and sent to Google Places to get the list of nearby venues.
  • We do not store your location or build a history of where you have been.
  • What is stored is the venue you pick and its city, because they are part of the dish you post and are shown publicly.

3. How we use your information, and our legal basis

We use your data to:

  • Show your profile and the dishes you have posted in the app.
  • Let other users see your ratings and photos.
  • Work out the dish ranking for your city and the weekly league.
  • Send you notifications about likes and ratings on your dishes.
  • Spot crashes and understand which parts of the app are used, so we can improve it.

If you live in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the GDPR requires us to tell you what we rely on legally for each of those uses:

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): your account, your profile, and posting dishes, ratings and comments. Without processing that data we cannot provide the service you are asking us for.
  • Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): location, notifications, access to your camera and your photos, and personalised advertising. You give these yourself and can withdraw them whenever you want, from your Android settings or from “Ad privacy options” in your profile.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): spotting crashes, preventing abuse, and understanding in aggregate how the app is used so we can improve it. You can object by writing to foodranker.app@gmail.com.

Withdrawing consent does not affect what we did lawfully before you withdrew it.

4. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your personal data or pass it to third parties for commercial purposes.
  • We do not publish your email address. It stays in Google’s authentication system and is not stored in the database other users read, so nobody can see it from the app.
  • We do not store your location or track you in the background.

5. Photos you upload and automatic review

When you upload a photo you confirm that:

  • You own the image or have permission to publish it.
  • You allow FoodRanker to display it inside the app.

Before it goes live, every photo is checked automatically with Google Cloud Vision to confirm it is food and has no inappropriate content. If it is not, it is rejected and never published. This check is automatic and no person reviews the image at that step.

Published photos are stored on Cloudinary and are publicly visible inside the app.

6. Third-party services

FoodRanker uses the following services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google) — sign-in, database, server functions, push notifications, usage analytics, crash reports (Crashlytics) and remote config.
  • Google Places and location services (Google) — nearby venue search and venue details.
  • Google Cloud Vision (Google) — automatic photo review.
  • Cloudinary — storage for published images.
  • Google AdMob (Google) — advertising. It may collect device identifiers to serve ads, under its own policy.
  • Google Play Billing (Google) — Premium subscription. Payment is handled by Google Play; FoodRanker neither receives nor stores your card details.

We recommend reading the privacy policy of each service.

7. Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. If you subscribe to Premium, ads stop showing. AdMob may use identifiers from your device to personalise ads; you can limit this from the Android privacy settings on your phone.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Access to your account is protected by Google Sign-In, and data that only the server should write (scores, XP, Premium status) cannot be changed from the app. Even so, no system is completely secure.

9. Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data for as long as your account is active.

  • You can delete any dish or rating you have posted at any time.
  • You can delete your account from your profile. Doing so removes your profile, your dishes, your ratings, your comments and your saved items.
  • Crash reports and usage analytics are anonymous or aggregated and may be kept for as long as Firebase retains them.

10. Your rights

At any time you can:

  • Access your data and correct your profile from the app.
  • Withdraw location or notification permission from the Android settings.
  • Delete the dishes and ratings you have posted.
  • Delete your account and all your data from your profile, or by writing to: foodranker.app@gmail.com

If you live in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to request a copy of your data, to object to its processing and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority: the AEPD in Spain, the ICO in the United Kingdom.

11. Children

FoodRanker is not aimed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will let you know about significant changes. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, write to us at:
