Delete your account and data

Last updated: November 2026

Two ways to delete

In FoodRanker you can delete your whole account, or delete only part of what you have posted and carry on using the app. Both are done from inside the app, and you do not need anyone's permission.

  • Delete your whole account — everything goes and the account disappears.
  • Delete only part of your data — a dish, a comment, a list.

Delete your whole account

  1. Open FoodRanker and sign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the screen, to the account area.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm in the dialog that appears.

Deletion is immediate and irreversible: there is no waiting period and no way to recover the account afterwards.

What is deleted with your account

The following is permanently deleted:

  • Your profile and profile picture.
  • Every dish you have posted, along with its photos.
  • All your ratings and comments, both your own and those other people left on your dishes.
  • Your followers, the people you follow, and your saved dishes.
  • Your lists.
  • The reports you have sent and your weekly league history.
  • Your notifications.
  • Your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication).

No personal data is retained after deletion. Crash reports and aggregated, anonymous usage statistics may be kept for Firebase's own retention periods, but they are not linked to your identity once the account is gone.

Delete only part of your data

You do not need to delete your account to remove something specific. From the app you can delete:

A dish you posted

Profile → My dishes tab → the dish's edit button → Delete.

This deletes the dish, its photo, every rating and comment it had (including other people's) and any saves pointing to it. The XP that dish earned you is taken back. It is immediate and irreversible.

A comment of yours

On the dish where you wrote it, swipe your comment to the left, or use the delete button on the comment itself. It only appears on comments that are yours.

One of your lists

Profile → Collections tab → delete the list. The list is removed; the dishes it held stay published, because they are not yours — they belong to whoever posted them.

A saved dish

Tap the save icon again to remove it from your saved dishes.

Someone you follow

From that person's profile, Unfollow.

What cannot be deleted on its own

The ratings you have given to other people's dishes. You can change the scores and the text whenever you like, but you cannot delete a single rating.

The reason is that each person can only rate a dish once: if ratings could be deleted, you could simply delete and vote again to get around that limit and move the ranking at will.

Those ratings do go when the dish they belong to is deleted, or when you delete your account.

If you do not have the app installed

You can request deletion of your account and data, or of part of it, by writing to foodranker.app@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. Confirm your identity and we will process the deletion within 30 days at most.
